## ⬇ How to play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below.
2. Double-click it. A console window opens and your browser loads the game.
3. Friends on the **same Wi-Fi / LAN** type the *"Same network"* address shown in that window (for example `http://192.168.1.20:3000`). They only need a browser.

> The first launch asks for network access: tick **Private networks**. SmartScreen may warn that the exe is unsigned: click *More info → Run anyway*.

There are no accounts and no internet servers. You can also add **bots** (Easy, Medium, Hard, Impossible) or **local players** for pass-and-play on one screen.

## What's in v0.2

- **Procedural maps** in six layouts: continent, supercontinent, twin continents, archipelago, inland sea, crescent isles.
- **Opening draft**: everyone picks 3 cards at the same time before round 1.
- **Resupply** each turn: take a card plus half your income, or take the full income. Duplicate units level up ★.
- **Supply Drop** cards (Rare / Epic / Legendary): a slot-machine reel picks a crate tier, then you reveal the crate.
- **Rarity-based reveals**: Commons snap over, Epics fly to centre stage, Legendaries shake, spin and detonate.
- **Chips × Mult combat**: you fling your own die into the tray (or press Space). Combined Arms, Pincers (shift-click stacks), Flanking.
- **Navy**: sea control, offshore oil rigs, shore bombardment, Ark transports, bridges across narrow straits.
- **Economy**: supply lines, Markets, Refineries, Depots, encirclement annexing, and trading with other players.
- **Five factions**, a 10-card unit deck, a card index and a full rules screen in game.

Full rules: [docs/RULES.md](https://github.com/Blacksheep909/sector/blob/main/docs/RULES.md)

`Sector-source.zip` holds the source if you'd rather run `node server.js` on any OS (Node 22+, no dependencies).
