## What's new in v0.5

**The sea is worth taking**, though it's never required: players who stay on land lose nothing.

- **Fishing grounds:** shoals with red buoys, worth +$1 each while you hold them.
- **Twice as many offshore oil rigs** (+$2 each).
- **Shipping lanes:** every Port earns $1 per 4 of your sea hexes connected to it, up to $5 per Port.
- **Treasure islands:** small islands now hold ruins, caches, villages and relics. Sail an Ark over to grab them.
- **Naval gunfire:** each warship next to a land fight adds +2 chips to its side (up to 3 ships), whether attacking or defending. Fleets now back up the land war.
- **Smarter bot admirals:** bots build Ports when there are prizes at sea, chase rigs and fishing grounds, and raid treasure islands.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.5.0 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
