## What's new in v0.5.5

- **Emergency muster.** If someone sits on your HQ, every hex you own next to it becomes a deploy point, and troops placed there can attack immediately. Rush in defenders and take your HQ back on the same turn. The siege warning tells you how, and bots use it too: in testing, about a third of besieged HQs were won back.
- **Fixed: land flashing between two colours during a siege.** While you sat on someone's HQ, your blueprint preview acted out their elimination, so their territory flickered between your colour and theirs. The preview no longer resolves sieges; they resolve for real at the start of your turn.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.5.5 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
