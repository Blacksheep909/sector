## What's new in v0.13.4

### 🎴 Supply drops open straight to their pack
No more slot-machine reel. A Supply Drop's rarity is the pack you get, every time: a **Rare** drop is a **Standard** pack, an **Epic** drop a **Gilded** pack, a **Legendary** drop a **Mythic** pack. Gilded and Mythic packs land in a burst of their light.

### ✋ Packs and cards feel better
- **A bigger tear strip.** The top seal of a pack is a third taller, so it's easy to grab and drag along. The TEAR tag is gone: the perforation and the notch show where to tear.
- **Smoother card tilt.** Cards lean toward your pointer with an eased glide and a gentler tilt, instead of jittering when you move slowly or circle the card.

### 🛠 For hosts
- **`tools/valor.js` gives a player Valor from the terminal** on a running server (with `SECTOR_DEV_KEY` set): `node tools/valor.js --list` to find them, `node tools/valor.js <name> 500` to give 500. See the Hosting guide.
