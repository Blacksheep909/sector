## What's new in v0.9.8

**Taking out a rival pays like it should.**

- **💰 Spoils of war scale with the game:** eliminating a commander used to pay a flat $10. Now the conqueror gets all of this, on top of the heartland:
  - $10 plus $2 per round played
  - the fallen commander's whole treasury
  - one turn of their income
  - up to 2 of their tactic cards

  The later the war, the bigger the prize. In testing, a round-20 elimination paid about $86 plus 2 cards.
- The fall banner and the log show what you plundered.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.9.8 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
