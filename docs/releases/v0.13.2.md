## What's new in v0.13.2

### 🤖 Bots play better, not richer
- **Bots earn what you earn.** No difficulty gets extra (or less) money any more: Easy used to make 75% of normal income and Impossible 150%. Difficulty is now only how well a bot plays.
- **They guard their HQ against what can actually reach it.** Every bot works out which enemy units could strike its HQ next turn: each unit's real move range, Grunts riding in carriers and helicopters, aircraft, and an extra hex of margin under fog of war. Hard bots look half a turn further ahead and Impossible bots a full turn. When the HQ can't hold, the bot pulls nearby troops home first, then buys the sturdiest defenders it can afford, and no attack or move takes the HQ below what it needs. Defensive units stay home, so the strikers still go to war.
- **They guard their FOBs and take them back.** A FOB with an enemy raid in reach gets a guard. A FOB you took off a bot, or planted against its land, becomes a top target: nearby stacks close in from every side and strike it together before you can start deploying from it.
- **They counterattack.** A stack that pushes into a bot's land, onto ground it just lost or hemmed in on most sides with little of its own army behind it, gets hit with an extra stack and at slightly worse odds. Harder bots counter more ruthlessly.
- **They think before they attack.** Medium and up weigh what enemy troops could reach a hex next turn before taking it, so they're less likely to win a hex and lose the stack on it a turn later.
- **The Iron Covenant digs in.** Iron bots attack less readily and guard their front harder, then hit back half again as hard when someone over-commits against them.
- **Anti-air where the aircraft are.** Bots count only enemy aircraft within striking distance of their land (not a rival's air force across the map), put new anti-air up at the base nearest them, and move it to cover the stacks actually under the air threat.
- **They finish their wars.** Bots keep most of their income for troops, keep slow tanks, guns and anti-air to a sensible share of the army, move fast units ahead of slow ones, bridge rivers on the way to the target's HQ, ferry troops stranded on islands, and take the ground round an enemy HQ before going for it. Bot-only wars that ran out of time dropped from about half to under 1 in 10.

### 🌫 Fog of war fixes
- Picking HQs under fog no longer shows the map or its prizes: only the spots you may pick glow.
- When a commander falls, only the part of their empire you can see changes hands on your screen.
- **Silent subs.** Under fog an enemy Kraken or Trident only shows up when one of your units is right next to it.

### ☢ Fallout means fallout
Nothing in a nuked or Tsar-bombed zone survives, nobody can hold it or deploy into it until it clears, and a base caught in a nuke is out of action. The Tsar Bomba now drifts down under a parachute and the nuke falls as a finned bomb.

### 🎮 Play
- **↶ Undo is one step.** Ctrl+Z takes back your last move, deploy or build only.
- **Encirclement fixes.** The map's edge and your own sea count as walls, and a small lake inside a pocket no longer stops it being annexed.
- **Airbase cap shown** in the build bar ("2/3").
- **Hover a stack, see its cards** in the hex tooltip.
- **Abyssal** movement 3 → 2.
- **Total War mission** retuned for the full-income enemy.
- **Fix:** knocking out a commander who was besieging someone else's HQ could leave that HQ's owner impossible to knock out.
- **Fix:** a knocked-out commander's minefields and buildings anywhere on the map are cleared with them.

### 🎨 Look and feel
- Map badges are drawn on top of scenery and borders.
- Burning bridge wrecks, a new kamikaze drone, and warship missiles that ripple out of launch cells and skim in over the water.
- Mythic cards are card-sized at last, and building tips are one line.
- New foil packs, and the burst of light when you tear one no longer shows as a box.
- A tidier player bar, a quieter Strategic Bombing summary, and a button to fold the chat away.
- Card skins now come only from the daily store and crates.

### ⚡ No more lag after super speed
When the client falls far behind it jumps to the latest state and only plays out the last few turns.
