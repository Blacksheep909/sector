## What's new in v0.6.5

- **Real weather.** Blizzards now bring drifting snow in layers (near flakes bigger and softly glowing), gusts of wind, and a frosty edge to the screen. Storms and rough seas bring slanted rain, a darker sky and, in storms, the odd lightning flash.
- **No free Ark.** Commanders start with Grunts and their faction unit. Only those whose HQ starts on a small island get an Ark, so nobody is stranded.
- **Embargo reworked:** click any enemy territory and that commander earns **half income for their next 2 turns** and **can't trade** while it lasts. It also has new art: a crate of gold chained shut.
- **Upgrade prices on hover.** In build mode, hovering a building you already own shows the upgrade price (e.g. Market ★ → ★★ $12) on the tooltip and on the build-bar tile.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.6.5 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
