## What's new in v0.10.6

### 🎯 Mission 20: Endgame (new tier: Nightmare)
Take a ★★★ fortress in three turns, behind a guarded bridge and an air-defence screen. Clear the bridge before your Ark can sail under it, knock out the Skyguard and Sentinel before the Bunker Buster goes in, then storm the HQ from two sides with your cards. Get the order wrong and it's close to hopeless.

### ✈ New effects
- **Kamikaze Drones dive in:** a delta-wing drone runs in low and flies straight into its target.
- **Anti-air fires missiles:** fast, direct missiles on white smoke trails, for anti-air units shooting at aircraft and for batteries intercepting air strikes and Trident missiles. A downed jet or drone blows up in mid-air.
- **No popup banners at Super and Max speed**, except the ones about you (your turn, your HQ under siege, a betrayal, an embargo).

### ⚓ Fixes and balance
- **Arks fixed:** troops who boarded an Ark could get stuck. The Ark now carries them whether you pick the Ark alone or the whole stack.
- **Ships sail under bridges:** a sea bridge no longer blocks the channel. Ships pass underneath (they can't stop there), unless enemy troops stand on the bridge.
- **Trident nerf:** missile range **4** (was 8), defence **4** (was 6).
- **Main menu fix:** the end-of-game MAIN MENU button always brings the front page back.
- The zoomed card shows the same price as the card in your hand (it ignored discounts like the Arms Fair).

### 🏅 Rewards
- **Leaving early pays** Valor for what you did up to then (kills and captures; no win or placing bonus, and nothing for what the bot does after).
- **A game counts** once you've played **5+ minutes** and destroyed **10+ enemy units** (it used to be 6 rounds).

### 🃏 Card tracking
- Every tactic card remembers where it came from. When an epic or better card is played, the log says so ("Morrow's Orbital Strike came from the black market (round 9)"), and picking one up shows in the log too.
- **The log scrolls:** scroll up over it to read older lines.

### 📱 Mobile (early)
Phone layouts (compact HUD, collapsible build bar and log, chat toggle) and landscape only on phones.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window (press **C** to copy it). On the front menu: **PLAY** for a quick war, **＋ PRIVATE ROOM** to host one and share its code, a **party** to go in together, or **Join the LAN lobby** for the classic one-lobby game.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.10.6 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
