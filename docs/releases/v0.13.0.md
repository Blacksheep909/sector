## What's new in v0.13.0

### 🎯 Five Situations: missions with a live enemy
After Endgame come five bigger missions where the enemy is a real bot with income and resupply, so there's no single solution, only a plan:
- **Breakout:** smash the gate town between your home army and a cut-off division.
- **Island Chain:** seize a bridgehead and the bunkered town beyond it.
- **Relief Force:** hold a besieged FOB for seven turns.
- **Two Fronts:** take a fortress while a second enemy marches on your capital.
- **Total War:** knock out a rival on open ground.

### 🎨 New art, after the real thing
- **Tanks:** Leviathan is a Leopard 2A7V, Goliath an M1A1 Abrams, Mauler a T-72B3 and Titan a T-14 Armata.
- **Fleet:** a top-down Ford-class Carrier with jets on deck, an amphibious landing ship (Ark), a Horizon-class destroyer (Marlin), a FREMM frigate (Bastion), an Admiral Gorshkov (Abyssal) and a Virginia-class submarine (Kraken).
- **Helicopters:** a UH-60 Black Hawk (Stormcrow), a V-22 Osprey (Valkyrie) and an Mi-24 Hind (Ravager).
- **Warships fire missiles** from their launch cells, and submarines send torpedoes in.

### 🤖 Bots finish wars
- **They cross rivers:** routes no longer run through unbridged rivers, and a bot cut off by one bridges the shortest crossing.
- **They stage forward:** FOBs go on the road to the target's HQ.
- **At sea:** empty Arks sail to the waiting army, and an overseas war makes the Ark card worth taking.
- **They keep cash for the battle cards** they hold.

### 🎯 Covered airspace, clearer
- Hovering, tapping or long-pressing a hex where your aircraft would stop inside enemy air defence now warns you how many launchers would fire.
- The **Sky Shield** mission teaches covered airspace and the anti-air weakness against ground troops.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window (press **C** to copy it). On the front menu: **PLAY** for a quick war, **＋ PRIVATE ROOM** to host one and share its code, a **party** to go in together, or **Join the LAN lobby** for the classic one-lobby game.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.13.0 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
