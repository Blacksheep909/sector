## What's new in v0.9.3

**Plan with your allies, and lend them troops.**

- **🗺 Ally war room:** press **Alt** to switch the war-room view on and off (it's a toggle now, not hold-to-see). In it you can:
  - drag from one hex to another to draw an arrow
  - click a hex to circle it
  - right-click to erase your marks there

  Only you and your allies can see the marks, and only in this view. Use it to plan pincers, call targets and mark danger while you wait.
- **🎁 Troop donations:** on your turn, select a stack and press **GIVE** on an ally's name card. The stack becomes theirs, ready to act on their turn, and bots like you more for it.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.9.3 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
