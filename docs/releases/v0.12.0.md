## What's new in v0.12.0

### 🤖 Smarter, deadlier bots
- **Troops first:** bots were spending more on black-market cards than on their armies, so late wars ran out the clock. Now they build armies, and **go for the kill**, harder the higher the difficulty: they pick on a weakened rival, march on its HQ and mass up to take it.
- **Every unit used for its job:** artillery holds at firing range and never parks next to the enemy, anti-air covers the army, empty troop helicopters fly to pick up Grunts, Carriers stand off the enemy coast with aircraft aboard, and **Arks ship armies across the sea** (about 5× as many landings).
- **Build behind the lines:** money-makers go well back from the front; FOBs go forward but never on the front line.
- **Play to their faction:** Volt flies, Ashen grabs land, Iron digs in, Verdant builds.
- **Fair fog of war:** with fog on, bots only know what a player in their seat would.

### 🎯 Air power and air defence
- **Covered airspace:** anti-air fires on its own at enemy aircraft that stop near it (once a turn), and Grunts aboard a downed helicopter go down with it. Covered airspace is shaded red while you fly.
- **Anti-air and artillery do their own jobs:** anti-air is almost harmless to ground troops (1 chip each) and artillery can't shell aircraft or brawl up close. Both are thin-skinned: keep them behind your line.
- **Every air strike can hit a bridge**, and **bridges take a turn to build**.
- **The right aircraft for the job:** B-52s for Strategic and Carpet Bombing, a B-2 for the Bunker Buster, an A-10 for the Gun Run, a bomber-dropped Tsar Bomba, and an Iskander-style Oracle launch.

### 🗺 Maps
- **New Tiny map size**, and **up to 10 commanders** a war (two new colours; the top bar goes compact in big wars).
- **Not every map is an island:** on many maps the land now runs off the edge of the board.
- **Rivers fixed:** one hex wide, unbroken to the sea, never cut in two by villages or relics.

### ⚖ Balance
- **Thumper** moves 2 (was 1). **Mule** defence 2. **Basilisk** defence 1, **Hailstorm** 1, **Oracle** 2.

### 🔧 Fixes
- **Card text fits on every card** (hover for the full rules), and **Anti-air** cards say so.
- **No false "Lost" alerts** after an undo, scrapping a unit or giving troops away.
- **Tactic cards with no target are greyed out**, not an error.
- **Space to skip** a fight; a **quiet opening draft** without a toast per pick; **calmer mythic cards**.
- **Honest odds** next to a bunker line in fog of war.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window (press **C** to copy it). On the front menu: **PLAY** for a quick war, **＋ PRIVATE ROOM** to host one and share its code, a **party** to go in together, or **Join the LAN lobby** for the classic one-lobby game.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.12.0 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
