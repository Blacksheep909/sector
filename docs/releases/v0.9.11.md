## What's new in v0.9.11

**Blitz through empty land, and a clean exit.**

- **⚡ Sweeping advance:** troops used to stop on every enemy hex they captured, so clearing out a beaten rival took forever. Now undefended enemy land doesn't slow you down. Your troops move at full speed through empty enemy hexes and capture every one on the way. They stop when:
  - they take a fortified hex (FOB or Flak)
  - they come next to enemy troops
  - they reach a river

  Bunkers and HQs still have to be attacked. Bots blitz too.
- **🚀 Max speed skips the dice:** on max speed, every fight (even the close ones, and crushing wins) plays as a quick flash on the map. The dice screen never stops the game.
- **Fix:** leaving a game while a move was still animating could paint the old board back behind the main menu. The menu now stays clean.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.9.11 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
