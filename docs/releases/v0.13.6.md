## What's new in v0.13.6

### 🧭 Player cards draw over the turn banner
v0.13.5 pushed the **⏳ NAME'S TURN** banner down to clear the current player's raised card. The banner is back where it was, and the top bar is now layered above it instead: the current player's card, with its **TRADE / ALLY** buttons, draws on top of the banner rather than hiding under it.
