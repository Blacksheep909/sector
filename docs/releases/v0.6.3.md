## What's new in v0.6.3

- **Real fog of war.** Turn it on in the lobby and the map starts dark.
  - **Unexplored land is hidden completely:** no terrain, owners, buildings or loot, just drifting cloud. Only HQ locations are known.
  - **Places you've explored but can't currently see** show the terrain and what you **last saw** there, dimmed, with no units.
  - **What your units, borders and allies can see** is fully live.
  - The game remembers what each commander has explored, so scouting finally matters.
- Garrison strengths on villages and forts are confirmed random per village and per game (villages 3–9, forts 3–8 with a ★–★★★ Bunker).

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.6.3 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
