## What's new in v0.13.3

### 🎴 Packs you tear open by hand
- **Tear it yourself.** Grab the top strip and drag: the foil peels up off the tear as one flap and follows your pointer (pull up to lift it), crackles as it rips (faster is louder), throws off flecks of foil, and leaks the light of the best card inside until it snaps off. A click only rips a small bite; Space or Enter still opens it, and it opens on its own after 20 seconds of nothing.
- **Booster-pack looks.** The pack's lead unit fills the face as key art, graded to the pack's tier. The cards inside push up a raised panel whose rim catches the light, and the foil creases where it meets the crimped seals. The pack tilts toward your pointer and its layers shift in depth. Better packs carry a sash saying what's guaranteed inside.

### 🤖 Bots
- **Bot income by difficulty.** Easy earns 75% of normal income, Medium 90%, Hard 110% and Impossible 150%.
- **They open fast.** For the first seven rounds, Medium and up spend on troops first, take full income over all but a strong card, and attack at slightly closer odds.
- **They fight as one army.** Hard and Impossible pick a rally point on the front, where the enemy is thin, and every stack not already fighting converges on it. New troops deploy toward it too.
- **They keep the pressure on.** Stacks out of contact close on the nearest enemy land, and a near-certain kill is never called off for fear of the reply.
- **They want the cards they need.** A bot reads your army against its own deck and goes for what it's missing: anti-air when you fly, a tank hunter against armour, siege guns against forts.
- **Impossible no longer over-guards** its HQ and FOBs at the expense of its army.

### 🎮 Play
- **The opening draft is units only.** Tactic cards come later, from resupplies, crates and the black market.
- **Teams: supply runs through your teammate's land,** so your hexes beyond theirs stay connected and pay income.
- **Teams: card ranges reach from your teammate's land** too (air strikes, missiles, nukes and the rest).
- **Public wars hold 10 with bots.** The host can fill a public war to 10 seats with bots; up to 6 humans still join through PLAY.
- **Discounted units say so.** When a unit costs less than its card (the Arms Fair world event, a faction perk), its price turns green and its tooltip says why.
- **The early resupply pick is gone** (it could pay twice), and **the black market no longer offers War Bonds**.
- **Stack tooltips show their cards side by side**, like a hand.

### 🔧 Fixes
- Units that couldn't fit on a captured hex snapped back to where they started the move; they now stay on the hex they attacked from.
- Finished wars (ended with END GAME, or with every human knocked out) are no longer offered under CONTINUE.
- No more bunkers in rivers (Verdant's starting bunker, the Fortify card).
- Helicopter rotors no longer draw as long dark sticks across neighbouring hexes; a spent helicopter shows its rotor stopped.
- The gold star over legendary units, which read as an upgrade level, is gone.
- "Need $51" when you had $51: with no room for a new building, the build bar now says so and quotes the upgrade price.
