## What's new in v0.13.10

### 🤖 Bots that fight for their lives
- **Bots take their HQ back.** When you sit on a bot's HQ, it now throws every unit in reach at it, even at long odds. Before, it wanted at least a 15% chance, so against a strong stack it walked its troops away and was knocked out next turn. (Easy bots still give up on hopeless counterattacks.)
- **Bots keep spending.** A bot whose HQ was packed full (5 units, or 10 with Grunts) had nowhere to deploy, so its money just piled up while you out-built it. Now it builds a FOB wherever the rules allow, or steps spare units off the HQ to make room. When the whole stack is guarding the HQ, it swaps the weakest unit out for something sturdier.
- **Bots stop resending orders the game refuses**, like driving a transport off and stranding its Grunts. They pick another move instead.

### 🏰 Sieges
- **A besieged HQ can always muster.** While your HQ is under siege, you can deploy on any land next to it with no enemy troops or forts on it, whoever owns it, and deploying there claims it back. Before, only land you still owned counted, so a besieger who also took the ring round your HQ left you no way to strike back.

### 💣 Strategic Bombing
- **Bombed land now really pays nothing for 2 turns**, as the card says. It used to come back after only one payday.

### 🗺 Tiny maps are actually tiny
- About **15–20 land hexes per commander** (was 20–31). A 2-player Tiny map has about 41 land hexes (was 63), a 6-player one about 98 (was 131). HQs can start 4 hexes apart.
- A crowded Tiny map grows a little rather than failing to generate.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.13.10 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
