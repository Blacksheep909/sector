## What's new in v0.13.5

### 🧭 The turn banner stays out of the way
The **⏳ NAME'S TURN** banner could slide over the current player's card in the top bar, hiding the bottom of that card and its **TRADE / ALLY** buttons. That happened most often when the player list wrapped onto a second row or the HUD was scaled down on a smaller screen. The banner now always sits just below the lowest player card, including the current player's raised one.

### ✋ Packs
- The tear strip is slim again, with a generous invisible grab zone over the top third of the pack.
