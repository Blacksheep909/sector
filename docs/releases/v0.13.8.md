## What's new in v0.13.8

### 🤝 Renewing an alliance ignores the size limit
An alliance bloc can hold at most half the commanders still standing. That limit used to block **RENEW** too, so when commanders fell, a pact you'd held for rounds would quietly lapse even though both of you wanted to keep it.

Now the limit only applies to **new** pacts. Renewing an existing one always works when both sides press RENEW, and bots renew their pacts the same way.
