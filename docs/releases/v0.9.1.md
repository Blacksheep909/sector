## What's new in v0.9.1

**Allies open their borders.**

- **🤝 Allied passage:** while you're allied, your troops and ships can cross your ally's land and sea, and stop on any of it that's empty. You never capture allied hexes. Hexes where your ally's troops stand can be crossed but not stopped on.
- **🚫 Don't get caught on the wrong side:** when a pact ends, by betrayal or by lapsing without renewal, any troops still standing in the other side's territory are lost. That applies both ways. A betrayal takes effect at the start of the breaker's next turn, and both sides get a warning to pull out first.
- **Bots** use allied land to pass through, but never park troops in it.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.9.1 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
