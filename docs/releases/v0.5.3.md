## What's new in v0.5.3

- **Supply Drops open like a real loot chest:**
  - Each tier has its own painted chest: oak and iron, red and gold, or violet crystal.
  - It slams down with a squash-and-bounce and a dust puff, then sits breathing and glowing until you tap it.
  - On the tap it hops three times while light leaks from the seam. Then the lid bursts open, a beam of light shoots up, and the sunburst spins behind it.
  - The three cards shoot out of the chest one by one while a counter ticks down.
- **Fixed:** the sunburst is now always centred on the chest. It used to fan in from the top of the screen.
- **Fixed:** the BREAKTHROUGH! / REPELLED! banner now lands over the finished dice tray instead of on top of the scores.
- **🎲 Random bot difficulty.** Set a bot to Random in the lobby, and it rolls Easy, Medium, Hard or Impossible when the game starts.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.5.3 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
