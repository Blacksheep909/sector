## What's new in v0.11.0

### 🗺 Map themes
- A new **Map theme** lobby option: Grasslands, Woodland, Desert, Badlands, Arctic, Jungle, Wetlands, Highlands or Mixed. **Random** (the default) rolls one for each war, and each theme changes what the land is made of.

### 🎴 Card packs
- **Supply drops and Store crates are now foil card packs**, torn open along the top seam and lit in the colour of the best card inside.
- **Rare reveals play out in full:** epic, legendary and mythic showcases finish before the next card turns over.

### 🃏 Cards you can read
- **Card text fits on the card** everywhere it's shown. Hover or zoom a card for the full rules.
- **Anti-air units say Anti-air** as their type.
- **Cleaner mythic cards:** the rainbow foil sweeps the frame, not the picture and the words.

### ⚙ Gameplay
- **Strategic Bombing really bombs:** each enemy unit under the bombs has a 30% chance to be destroyed.
- **Airbases are capped at 3**, and a new one must be at least 3 hexes from your HQ.
- **Nothing can be built in a river** except a Bridge.
- **Bots see a broken alliance coming:** they pull their troops out of the ally's land and guard their HQ against it.
- **Bots no longer strike blind** in fog of war.

### 🎮 Menus
- **Singleplayer has its own screen:** CONTINUE your latest save, or set up a quick war against bots.
- **Recruit friends from the front page.**
- **The pause menu fits the war:** missions show RESTART and QUIT MISSION.
- **Esc closes menu panels**, and the Locker and Store keep your place when you equip or buy something.

### 🎬 Look and feel
- **Special strike animations:** the Cruise Missile skims in low and dives, and artillery always shows the right shot for the guns firing.
- **Animations keep pace** with Fast and Super speed.
- **New Oracle art:** an Iskander-style missile launcher truck.
- **The fight panel always adds up**, and ranged shots say HIT or MISS.

### 🔧 Fixes
- **Clicking the map always works:** invisible boxes were swallowing clicks.
- **END TURN works first time.**
- **No more FOBs stuck under construction** after an undo or loading a save.
- **Singleplayer saves give you your own seat back.**
- **After the war:** WAR OVER in the top bar, the HUD steps aside, and the victory screen is never cut off.
- **The map can't be dragged off screen.**
- **Safari:** the HUD no longer slides half off small Mac, iPhone and iPad screens.
- **DEPLOY is always on screen** on laptop-height screens.
- **Neater spacing** on the alliance offer and the SERVER button.
- **Long callsigns are handled cleanly** in the party list.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window (press **C** to copy it). On the front menu: **PLAY** for a quick war, **＋ PRIVATE ROOM** to host one and share its code, a **party** to go in together, or **Join the LAN lobby** for the classic one-lobby game.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.11.0 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
