## What's new in v0.9.7

**See your friends' land, and tidier bridges.**

- **🤝 Allied land stands out:** every hex your allies hold has a dashed green line just inside its border, so you can see all your friends' territory at a glance. In fog of war, allying with someone also reveals their whole territory and their HQ.
- **🌉 Bridge fixes:**
  - You can't build a bridge on top of an oil rig.
  - The "no bridges side by side" rule now covers river bridges too.
  - If you see bridges sprawling across neighbouring hexes, that's the old bridge model from before v0.9.4. Update (press **U** in the server window) to get the new one, which stays inside its own hex.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.9.7 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
