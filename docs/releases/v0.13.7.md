## What's new in v0.13.7

### ⭕ Encirclement happens where you close the ring
After every move, deploy, build or card, the game used to search the **whole map** for pockets to annex. So you could sail a boat on one side of the board and suddenly gain hexes on the other side, from a pocket that had been walled in earlier (say, when an enemy unit walked out of it).

Now a pocket is only annexed by the action that **closes it**: the hexes you just won have to be part of the ring. Moving a boat only encircles things next to where that boat claimed sea. A pocket that's already walled in but wasn't taken (an enemy was standing in it, for example) flips once you claim another hex on its edge.
