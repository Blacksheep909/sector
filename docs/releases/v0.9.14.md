## What's new in v0.9.14

**The blueprint holds still.**

- **Fix: blueprint flicker.** While you planned during someone else's turn, the board swapped between the real board and your blueprint every time an enemy unit moved. Your planned stacks, captures and deploys blinked out and back, and tiles faded between colours. Now the board always shows the real state, and your blueprint is drawn on top in light blue:
  - ghost stacks where your orders put them
  - a dashed blue wash on land you'll capture
  - ghost buildings where you'll build
- **Fix:** the blueprint's dashed ring was drawn off-centre on stacks sharing a hex with a building, such as your HQ.
- **Fix:** the blueprint order list could get squeezed to one line by the alerts panel. It now keeps room for your orders, and the alerts panel scrolls instead.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window. Press **C** to copy it.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.9.14 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
