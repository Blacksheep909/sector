## What's new in v0.10.7

### 📱 SECTOR on your phone
- **Android app:** download **`SECTOR-android.apk`** below and open it on your phone (Android asks to allow installs from that source the first time). Type the **INVITE** address Sector.exe shows (same Wi-Fi) once; it remembers it and reconnects next time. Landscape only, full screen, and the screen stays awake. **⇄ SERVER** on the front page switches servers.
- **Install from the browser:** on a server reached over HTTPS, phones offer **Install app** (Android) or **Add to Home Screen** (iPhone), and SECTOR opens full screen in landscape with its own icon.
- **Phones held sideways:** a bigger, more readable HUD, a pause menu that fits, and the resupply, draft, crate, black market and fight screens all fit a phone's height.

### 🤝 Teams play as a team
- **Deploy at a teammate's FOB** and launch ships from their Ports.
- **Open hands:** see your teammates' cards and cash. Press **🃏 TEAM** under a teammate's name to **send them cash** or **pass them a card**, instantly.
- **Team chat** (TEAM / ALL switch, or Tab), and **map pings only reach your own side**.
- **One colour per team**, and each team's land in the top bar. Bot teammates help out a teammate who's run out of cash.

### ⚙ Gameplay
- **Helicopters claim oil rigs** by landing on them (Wren, Ravager, Banshee, Stormcrow, Mule, Valkyrie).
- **Upgrades show what they do:** an UPGRADE card on offer shows the change, like ⚔5→6 🛡4→5.

### 🎨 Look and feel
- **Seraph** redrawn as an AC-130 from above.
- **Black market button** redrawn in the game's own style.
- **Opening draft no longer jumps** up and down when the cards are dealt (Supply Drops too).
- **Blueprint orders** lose the blue strip down their side.
- **Pause menu** goes two columns on laptop and phone screens, so it never runs off the bottom.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window (press **C** to copy it). On the front menu: **PLAY** for a quick war, **＋ PRIVATE ROOM** to host one and share its code, a **party** to go in together, or **Join the LAN lobby** for the classic one-lobby game.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.10.7 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
