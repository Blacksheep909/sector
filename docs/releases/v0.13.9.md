## What's new in v0.13.9

### ⚖️ Nothing moves 5 any more
The four fastest units drop from **5 to 4 movement**:

| Card | Type | Rarity | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Outrider** | Vehicle | Rare | 5 → **4** |
| **Wren** | Helicopter | Rare | 5 → **4** |
| **Phantom** | Aircraft | Epic | 5 → **4** |
| **Seraph** | Aircraft | Legendary | 5 → **4** |

Their other stats and costs are unchanged. The Outrider is now level with the Skitter, Jackal and Coyote as the fastest things on the ground. Faction perks, upgrades and cards like Blitz Doctrine still add movement on top.
