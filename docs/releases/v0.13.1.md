## What's new in v0.13.1

### 🏆 Alliance wins name every winner
When allies or a team win together, the victory screen, the log and the toast now read "Alice & Bob won the war together as allies" instead of crediting one player and listing the other underneath.

### 🤖 Smarter bots
- **They fight for their HQ.** Under siege, a bot keeps mustering the hardest hitters it can afford next to its HQ until the counterattack has good odds, then strikes straight away. In testing, bots now retake a besieged HQ 12 times out of 12 (it was 2 or 3).
- **They use Combined Arms.** Bots buy a balanced army: guns stay at most about a third of it, and they buy whichever of infantry, armour and aircraft they're short of. They join stacks that lack their kind of unit and mass mixed blows. Attacks bringing 3 kinds of unit went from about 5% to 16%.
- **They press the kill.** The more a bot outguns its target, the harder it drives on that HQ. Once it has an HQ under siege it piles on and takes the land round it, where the owner would muster.
- **Fewer idle troops.** Reserves no longer sit in the rear when the war is across a river: they march to the crossing.

### ✈ Aircraft claim the sea
Planes and helicopters take the sea hex they end their move over (an oil rig included), just like land.

### 🚁 Helicopters off Carriers
Planes and helicopters alike deploy straight onto a Carrier with room; the card and the deploy prompt now say so.

### ⚖ Balance and fixes
- **Hornet** attack 2 → 1.
- The "Player X's turn" banner sits below the player chips, so it no longer covers the ally and trade menu.
- A bridge under construction is drawn half-built behind a striped barrier, and its tooltip says it's ready next turn.

### 🎨 New icons
Scorpion is a **Gepard**, Hellcat an **M10 Booker**, Wraith a **BTR-82A**, Bulwark a **Bradley**, and the Trident an **Ohio-class submarine** launching an ICBM.

## ⬇ Download and play

1. Download **`Sector.exe`** below and double-click it. The **SECTOR server window** opens: a live dashboard with your invite addresses, players, game status and updates.
2. Press **O** to open the game in your browser, or go to **http://localhost:3000**. Everyone plays in the browser.
3. Friends on the same Wi-Fi open the **INVITE** address shown in the server window (press **C** to copy it). On the front menu: **PLAY** for a quick war, **＋ PRIVATE ROOM** to host one and share its code, a **party** to go in together, or **Join the LAN lobby** for the classic one-lobby game.
4. Already on v0.4 or later? The server window says **v0.13.1 is out — press U** and updates itself.

> **Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info → Run anyway**. SECTOR isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Microsoft Defender scans it clean, and in-app updates (press **U**) don't trigger the warning again. The first launch also asks for network access: tick **Private networks** so friends can connect.
